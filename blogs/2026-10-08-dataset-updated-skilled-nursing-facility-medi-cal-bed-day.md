This dataset provides Medi Cal Bed Day (MCBD) counts for California Freestanding Skilled Nursing Facilities (FS/SNFs) completed by the Skilled Nursing Facility Accountability Sanctions Program (SNF ASP). It includes each facility’s identifiers (Facility ID, Facility Name, HCAI ID, and NPIs), monthly MCBD counts, and total annual MCBD counts. Some entries are suppressed in accordance with the DHCS Data De identification Guidelines (DDG) v3.0.
